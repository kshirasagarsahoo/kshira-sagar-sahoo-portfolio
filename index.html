About Me

Dr. Kshira Sagar Sahoo is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at SRM University-AP, India. He is a Senior Member of IEEE and was a Kempe Postdoctoral Fellow (2022–2024) at Umeå University, Sweden. His research focuses on Software Defined Networking (SDN), Software Defined Internet of Things (SD-IoT), Cyber Security, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Edge and Cloud Computing, Distributed Systems, and Intelligent Network Management.

Dr. Sahoo received his Ph.D. from the National Institute of Technology (NIT), Rourkela in 2019, his M.Tech. from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Kharagpur in 2014, and his B.Tech. from ITER, Bhubaneswar (Siksha 'O' Anusandhan University) in 2007. Before joining SRM University-AP, he served as an Assistant Professor at VNR Vignana Jyothi Institute of Engineering and Technology (VNRVJIET), Hyderabad and Madanapalle Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Andhra Pradesh.

His research contributions span network security, scalable SDN architectures, intrusion detection systems using machine learning, cloud-native computing, edge intelligence, Internet of Things, and distributed computing. He has authored numerous high-impact research papers in leading IEEE, ACM, Elsevier, Springer, and Wiley journals, receiving thousands of citations for his work on DDoS attack detection, SDN security, cloud computing, and AI-enabled networking.

Dr. Sahoo is actively involved in research collaboration, academic mentoring, patent development, and international scientific communities. His current interests include Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, IoT Security, Distributed Computing, Software Defined Networks, Edge Computing, Explainable AI, and Intelligent Cyber Defense Systems.
